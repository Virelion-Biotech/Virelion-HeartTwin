BodyParts3D heart
BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International.
An adult male reference surface, with 11 selectable anatomical groups. It includes atrial walls, a combined ventricular wall, ventricular cavity surfaces, major vessel segments and coronary artery trees. Cavity surfaces represent spaces, not muscle tissue.
HeartTwin uses the OrganUI curated conversion of BodyParts3D 4.0. We simplified the display surfaces, restored millimetre scale, rounded coordinates and adjusted illustrative colors. The ventricular wall remains combined.
This is a static anatomical reference, not a simulation-ready volume mesh or a patient-specific model. Some structures are omitted and vessel ends are truncated. No activation or physiological result is supplied.
