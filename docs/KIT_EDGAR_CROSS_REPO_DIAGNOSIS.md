## Still required

KIT prediction remains inadequate under the tested setup. Further modelling needs qualified initial/reference states, independent physiological evidence, more informative pressure/flow targets and new patient/condition data. CardiMech still needs scalable spatial solving and convergence qualification for the supplied mesh. EDGAR still needs waveforms, verified coordinate/reference metadata and reviewed nonmanifold geometry. Software tests do not resolve these scientific/data limitations.
